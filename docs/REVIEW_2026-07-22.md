## 1. Verdict

This is an unusually disciplined research repository. The central discipline — refusing to let a striking
observation acquire causal or biological language without the experiment that licenses the promotion — is
precisely what the genomic-foundation-model interpretability literature currently lacks; that field still
rests largely on attention-weight visualization and linear probing.

**The "not publishable yet" framing is too pessimistic.** Two publishable results plus a methods
contribution exist *now*, scoped honestly. The real gaps are **formalization**, **one generalization
experiment**, and a **mechanical cleanup pass** — none of them a science problem.

## 2. What a reviewer will stumble on

### 2.1 "Circuit" vs. minimality
The most interesting and counter-intuitive result is that the named circuit is **not minimal**
(F ≈ 0.005 scrubbed; ~72/103 nodes for 90%; no knee). A skimming reviewer reads "circuit" and expects a
sparse graph. `docs/METHODOLOGY.md:7-10` already defines the term correctly, but the definition does not
travel with the word.

**Required fix:** make **"structured causal core within an irreducibly distributed computation"** the
standing phrase at first use in every top-level document.

### 2.2 The A0→A12 file namespace is opaque
`a1…a12`, `ic1`, `preflight_*`, `a9c_sweep_seeded_draws` mean nothing without `DECISIONS_A0.md`. A reader
landing in `results/splicebert_coupling/` cannot tell canonical from exploratory from superseded.

### 2.3 10.63 (model) vs 10.298 (nucleosomal)
Easy to misread as "the model found nucleosome positioning" — the exact opposite of the finding. The
figures handle this well (fig4C); secondary prose must never state the two numbers without the
"0.33 nt apart, powered refutation" qualifier **in the same sentence**.

### 2.4 Two checkpoints
The donor circuit is on SpliceBERT.1024nt; the ≈10.63 carrier work involves the 510nt multi-species
checkpoint. This is stated in figure captions but not prominent in `MECHANISM.md`. One explicit line
prevents a reviewer inferring conflation.

## 3. Especially interesting, and why

- **The no-knee faithfulness curve (C27/C33) is the most valuable finding and it is undersold.** A
  circuit that is necessary and sufficient-when-injected, yet 99.5% non-faithful under scrubbing because
  *generation* is distributed, is a direct empirical instance of the sufficiency ≠ faithfulness ≠
  minimality tension the field is actively arguing about. Most circuit papers report a sparse graph and
  one faithfulness number; this reports the whole curve and shows there is no sparse explanation. That is
  a headline, not a caveat.
- **The powered refutation of the nucleosome bridge (C14).** Killing your own most attractive biological
  story with a calibrated assay is what makes a reviewer trust everything else in the ledger.
- **The address/payload factorial (C23).** Near-pure interaction (address-only +0.01, payload-only +0.05,
  joint +0.37) is a genuinely satisfying "how": a bind-and-convert operation, not a lookup.
- **The R1–R24 retraction ledger** is itself a teaching contribution to the subfield.

## 5. The one experiment that most de-risks review

**Cross-example / cross-context transduction (G1).**

Nearly all donor-graph causal evidence is intra-example clean/corrupt patching on short introns — which by
construction speaks only to that prompt distribution. A reviewer *will* ask whether the circuit generalizes
or whether patching merely works within matched pairs. `docs/METHODOLOGY.md:85-88` already states this
requirement; the study has not yet met it.

`MECHANISM.md` and `docs/ROADMAP.md` already specify the decisive test: derive `U_info` on discovery genes,
inject into **gene-disjoint** matched targets, require dose-ordered margin movement above matched-random and
within-example controls. The audited Head–Context Atlas supplies the matched cohorts.

Running even a modest version converts *"a mechanism on the tested distribution"* into *"a transferable
state"* and removes the single most likely rejection reason. Per house convention 1, this needs a frozen
preregistration before any compute — drafting that prereg is in scope for this program; **running it is not.**

## 6. Making the method publication-grade

The evidence ladder is a real contribution but reads as a bespoke local framework. Ground it in the field's
formal vocabulary — every item below is an *addition*, not a rewrite:

1. **Adopt the faithfulness-curve formalism** (MIB-style CPR/CMD, area under faithfulness-vs-size). The
   `a12` no-knee / 72-of-103 result *already is* this object; naming it is free credibility.
2. **Frame "circuit" in causal-abstraction terms.** This unifies activation/path patching, causal
   scrubbing, and mediation, and supplies the precise *graded faithfulness* notion the ladder already
   measures column-by-column. This single change most directly answers "the formalization isn't
   publication-grade yet."
3. **State the causal-scrubbing relationship explicitly** — C27's scrub-to-corrupt test is
   scrubbing-adjacent; say where it follows and where it deviates.
4. **Cite activation-patching best practices.** The study already uses in-distribution symmetric
   corruption, a continuous margin metric, and separate necessity/sufficiency tests. It is compliant —
   make that legible in Methods.
5. **Add an automated edge-attribution cross-check** (ACDC/EAP-style). Showing an automated pass recovers
   the same top-ranked core blunts "you found the circuit you went looking for."
6. **Turn the seven-stage workflow + tool-to-question map into the methods hero figure.**

> **Citation integrity gate.** Every reference added under §6 must be independently verified to exist and
> to say what it is cited for. Given this project's standards, a hallucinated citation would be worse than
> no citation. Unverified → omit.

## 7. Bridging interpretability to genomic phenomena

- **L3H1 ↔ splice-site pairing.** A donor scored by its *specific* paired acceptor across a 60–150 nt
  intron is the model implementing cross-intron communication — squarely the **intron-definition** regime.
  This is the most defensible biological bridge in the project and it is currently under-stated. Connect
  coupling strength to known determinants (5′/3′SS complementarity, intron-length limits).
- **Period-3 ↔ codon 3-periodicity.** Candidate *origin* = codon-composition periodicity in the coding
  corpus (the basis of Fourier ORF-finding); C36's phase-aligned CDS use is consistent. The over-reach is
  already ruled out: `U_info` is phase-**independent** (C19), so the present-day role is positional
  addressing, not reading-frame encoding. Correct formulation: **candidate origin = codon periodicity;
  proven role = relative-positional routing; origin unproven pending training-time evidence.** Do not
  claim coding enrichment — C36's contrast was inconclusive, and the repo correctly does not claim it.
- **≈10.63 ↔ nucleosome/helical periodicity — REFUTED, and that is the result.** The obvious ~10.2–10.5 bp
  bridge is excluded (10.298 real vs 10.63 model, 0.33 nt apart, powered). Publication statement: obvious
  bridge excluded, true correlate unknown, no claim. The open question — what 10.63-nt periodicity is
  *neither* codon *nor* nucleosomal — is a future-work hook, not a hole.

**General principle worth foregrounding:** a GFM circuit can have a clear bridge (L3H1), a
candidate-but-unproven origin (period-3), and a refuted obvious bridge with unknown true correlate
(≈10.63). Presenting all three *at their correct evidence levels* is more credible than the current
literature's habit of forcing a biological story onto every feature.

orange), narrative in captions rather than artwork, and the
`make_fig_report_data.py` → validated-package → `make_figN.py` pipeline with recompute-checks is exactly
right. **Keep** fig1's "two stories" layout and fig4C (the refutation zoom) — the two most persuasive
panels.

Recommendations:

  reproducible) rather than hand-placement.
- Keep the 3D carrier surface for talks, but pair a **2D heatmap** for the paper body — 3D is hard to read
  quantitatively.
- Add the **evidence-ladder hero figure** (§6.6).
- Standardize **one style module** (fonts, palette, DPI, PDF+PNG) imported by every `make_figN.py`.

No tool change needed: matplotlib + graphviz + a shared style token file, plus a grid composer for the
multi-panel composite.
