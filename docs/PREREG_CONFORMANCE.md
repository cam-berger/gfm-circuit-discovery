# Preregistration ↔ implementation conformance

> **Tier: T1 — canonical.** Current, authoritative. Read this.

**What this document is.** For every preregistered experiment family **in the SpliceBERT arc**, a
record of where the **executed code** departed from the **frozen protocol**, and whether any live
claim's wording depends on the gap. It exists because a reviewer's hardest question about a
preregistered project is not "what did you find" but "did you do what you said you would" — and until
this file existed, that question had no answer here short of reading 34 protocols against 150 runners.

> **Coverage gap, stated rather than hidden.** The **DNABERT-2 reconstruction atlas** — seven frozen
> protocols under `studies/dnabert2_reconstruction_atlas/` (`PREREGISTRATION.md` and `_D12_`
> through `_D17_`) — has **not** been through this audit. Its known protocol-vs-execution departures
> are self-reported in that study's `DECISIONS.md` and in the amendment sections of the individual
> preregistrations: D12 Amendment 1 (endpoint definitions chosen after inspecting the development
> split), D14's pre-run underpowered declaration for P2, D15 Amendment 1 (a memorial that could not
> fail, plus population memorials being gated at smoke scale), and D17's frozen decision rule being
> architecture-forced for L11H2 and run before the defect was found. Self-reported is not
> independently audited. **Do not read this file's totals as covering that arc.**
>
> That study was removed from the working tree in the 2026-07 cleanup and survives in Git history only, so
> the protocols and `DECISIONS.md` named above are not checkable in a current checkout. The coverage gap
> is unchanged by the removal: those departures were never independently audited, and this paragraph
> remains the only in-tree statement that they exist.

**Scope and method.** This audit was commissioned by the 2026-07-24 publication-readiness review, which
returned *Major Revision — not ready for external artifact review* and named this table its single
highest-leverage deliverable. 25 SpliceBERT-arc families audited (every frozen protocol on disk for
that arc plus the two unfrozen ones,
marked as such), each by an independent agent reading the protocol in full, then the runner(s) in full,
then the resulting artifact, the `CLAIMS.md` row, and any memorial test. Each checked eight things:
estimand, preregistered gates, memorials, verdict rule, sample size/split/seeds, controls and nulls,
amendment coverage, and claim consistency. Findings were then adversarially verified against HEAD.
**188 deviations, of which 157 are medium-or-worse.** Full per-finding evidence, including the
IMPLEMENTED/DEVIATES/ABSENT tables, is preserved outside the repo at
`gfm-circuit-discovery-review-2026-07-24/conformance_by_family.json`.

## Read this before the table

**No scientific result changes.** Not one deviation below overturns a finding. Where a gate was
mis-specified the conclusion is typically threshold-independent — A6's is the clearest case: the runner
applies an unpreregistered floor 22.7× the stored band, forcing `verdict.complete=true`, yet the largest
add-one gain over 93 non-circuit nodes is 0.498% of the residual and moves `F` from 0.0054 to 0.0104,
about 1% of the way to the preregistered `F ≥ 0.80` bar. What is at risk throughout is the **legibility of
the discipline**, which is the thing this repository exists to demonstrate.

**Exactly one deviation was logged before this audit.** R28 (G1's planted-positive gate). Everything else
below was undisclosed. That ratio — 1 of ~30 at the time of the audit — is the finding, more than any
individual row.

**The root cause is structural, not carelessness.** The decision layer of every experiment lives in the
**runner**, not in the protocol, and nothing checked the two against each other. Five shapes recur:

1. **Unpreregistered operational constants.** A protocol states a rule in words — "above the 95th
   percentile of the null", "≈ 0", "significant", "systematic/monotonic" — and the runner supplies a
   number that was never preregistered. A6 `max(add_p95, 0.05)`; A10 renders "≈0" as `<0.10`; A9b renders
   "significant" as `CV-R² ≥ 0.10`; A12 renders "knee" as `|K|_90 ≤ 40`; A8's gate is `[0.28, 0.45]`
   against a frozen `[0.30, 0.45]`.
2. **Preregistered arms never executed, nowhere logged.** B1's OV arm substituted a coupling-margin value
   main for the frozen `U_info`/`S_ov` endpoint (**R31**); SEALED dropped both faithfulness control arms;
   A10's sufficiency confirm never ran; G1's balance table is absent; DONOR_ANCHOR's "≥30 nt from *any*
   annotated splice site" screen checks only the donor and its mate.
3. **Preflight gates that cannot fail.** `checkpoint_recorded {"PASS": True}` in **16** runners; A1's
   `full_matches_A0` is literal `True`; A5's `disjoint` is `X∩(Y−X)=∅`; P3's `carrier_reconstruction` is
   `(a−b)+b−a`. Live prose then credits "preflight 8/8", "9/9", "eleven instrument gates". This is the
   project's own R7/R21 pathology, recurring.
4. **Hand-written verdicts in committed results.** Three artifacts (**R33**).
5. **Halt semantics that do not halt.** Protocols specify `Global PASS = AND(...)` with "run STOPS on
   failure"; the runners compute the gate and write the result regardless.

**How to use it.** A row here is a disclosure, not a to-do. Do **not** edit a frozen preregistration to
agree with the code — deviations are disclosed beside the protocol, never inside it. Do **not** edit the
runners to insert missing gates: their producing commits are pinned by the manifests beside their results,
so a "fix" desynchronises a provenance chain to repair a documentation defect. Fix forward in successor
runners; where a claim's *wording* depends on a gap, that is an R-number, and the ones already issued are
named inline below.
