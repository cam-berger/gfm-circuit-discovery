NOTEBOOK 9 — PATHWAY AND MEDIATION VALIDATION

Primary conclusion:
A low-dimensional promoter-associated intervention at the Layer-1 MLP propagates through a strongly transforming and non-additive downstream computation. Layer-4 attention and the Layer-5 MLP form the most compact validated downstream core, jointly mediating approximately 69% of the intervention-induced Layer-5 diagnostic-probe effect in both promoters and non-promoters.

Candidate pathway:
Layer-1 MLP promoter-associated subspace -> distributed Layer-2 and Layer-3 transformation -> Layer-4 attention -> Layer-5 MLP -> Layer-5 diagnostic representation

Compact validated downstream core:
Layer-4 attention branch -> Layer-5 MLP branch

Promoter compact-core mediation: 0.697 [0.646, 0.748]
Non-promoter compact-core mediation: 0.689 [0.635, 0.744]

Key limitation:
These results concern promoter-associated information measured by a fixed Layer-5 diagnostic probe; they do not establish a complete native DNABERT-2 promoter-classification circuit.
