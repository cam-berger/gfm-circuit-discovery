Target: ~11 slides, 16:9, elegant/minimal, one idea per slide, explanatory captions.
Palette: deep navy #12355B (clock/established), amber #C8860D (carrier/open), slate grays.

1. TITLE — project name, one-line thesis: "From model behavior to causally-supported mechanism, with an honest stopping line." SpliceBERT as first worked example.
2. THE PROBLEM — GFMs make biological predictions we can't explain. Attribution/attention maps generate hypotheses but don't establish mechanism. Need discipline: causal intervention decides.
3. THE METHOD — the evidence ladder (P -> QK -> Attention -> OV/Mediation -> Behavior -> Biology). Evidence never auto-promotes. Matched nulls, exact permutation p, retraction ledger.
4. THE BEHAVIOR — SpliceBERT scores a masked donor higher when its paired acceptor is intact. +0.872 coupling, partner-specific (non-partner +0.007). (C1-C2)
5. THE CIRCUIT — end-to-end causal graph: acceptor -> L3H1 -> MLP3/4 -> U_info -> transporters (L4/L5) -> MLM head. Meet-in-the-middle. (fig: circuit graph)
6. NECESSITY & MEDIATION — C={L3H1,L2H15}, 81.6% removed / 99.2% health, p=0.0025; conjunctive read; distributed readout chain. (fig3_circuit.png)
7. THE INFORMATION OBJECT — U_info: rank-8 direction recovers 0.92 (random <=0.05, p=0.005); stable on held-out genes (0.875); content = paired-intron consistency + donor identity, phase-independent. (fig2_mechanism.png)
8. TWO CARRIERS, TWO STORIES — period-3 clock reaches Behavior (coupling 0.946->0.027); ~10.63 carrier reaches Attention only (8 heads), function unknown. Nucleosomal 10.298 is distinct. (fig1_summary.png)
9. WHAT WE DID NOT CLAIM — the honest stopping line + 20 retractions (R19 zero-hit floor, R20 geometry-not-identity). Discipline as the deliverable.
10. VERIFICATION — independent double-check: 52/52 headline numbers match artifacts; memorial tests 120 passed; anomaly correctly nulled by correction.
11. THE FORWARD PROPOSAL — 3 aims: (I) push 10.63 carrier to OV/behavior; (II) A6 faithfulness/completeness battery; (III) transfer the ladder to a 2nd GFM, test for recurring motifs. Closing thesis.
