## Reason for amendment

V1 failed directional family coverage: 83/1,000 misses versus a frozen maximum of
71, independently reproduced from all consumed null raw payloads. There was no
measurement, truth-definition, or arithmetic discrepancy. The failure includes
both quantization-scale and ordinary-scale contrasts. No CC or model-forward
outcomes were exposed.

For iid block endpoint vectors with common within-stratum population covariance,
the uncorrected stratified empirical bootstrap has expected covariance ratio
`(N−H)/N = (342−32)/342 = 0.9064327485380117`. The following correction addresses
that specific finite-stratum variance bias. It does not by itself guarantee nominal
coverage or prove the complete cause of the v1 failure.

## Single declared correction

For every endpoint vector x within stratum h of size n_h≥2, let

`y_hi = xbar_h + s_h (x_hi − xbar_h)`, where `s_h = sqrt(n_h/(n_h−1))`.

Use exactly the original 20,000 whole-block resampling indices generated by seed
20260803, now applied to y. A block's entire endpoint vector receives the same
factor; retain paired arms, native/cell contrasts, protected-health summaries and
their covariance. Point estimates use the original x, and are unchanged because
each transformed stratum mean equals its original mean. No blocks are dropped,
reweighted in the estimand, merged across strata or replaced.

For count c_bhi of block i in replicate b, the equivalent coefficient on x_hi is
`[s_h c_bhi + (1−s_h)]/N`. These are coefficients of centered-residual replicates,
not sampling probabilities; negative coefficients when c_bhi=0 are intentional.
All replicate stratum weights remain n_h/N. The conditional covariance of the
overall replicated mean is `sum_h n_h S_h / N²`, where S_h is the within-stratum
sample covariance with denominator n_h−1.

Use these same corrected replicates for **every** scalar percentile interval,
simultaneous max-absolute-studentized interval, native positive-control bound and
native floor, protected-health bound, paired target-minus-control bound, and
centered MDE80 calculation. Preserve the fixed bootstrap-SD studentization and all
quantile levels. Do not suppress low-variance endpoints, add a variance floor,
switch interval algorithms, or change the truth-comparison tolerance of 1e−10.

The corrected analytic MDE diagnostic is
`(1.96+0.8416212335729143)*sqrt(sum_hi s_h²*(x_hi−xbar_h)²)/N`.
The gate remains the actual 20,000-draw corrected centered MDE≤0.05. Retain the old
analytic diagnostic under an explicitly uncorrected name. Empirical permutation
control means, matching/phase-blind means and exact add-one p-values do not use the
bootstrap and remain unchanged.
