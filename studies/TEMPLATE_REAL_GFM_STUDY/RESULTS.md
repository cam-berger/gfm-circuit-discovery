## Conventions

- Never overwrite a result after it has informed a decision.
- Supersede with a new artifact and preserve the old one.
- Every row links to a manifest with checkpoint, data, parameters, seed, code revision, and command.
- Figures link back to the exact rows/artifacts they display.
