## Corrected interpretation of the 96-head branch

The screen was exploratory, ran on train+val, and has a site-type-specific coordinate defect. Only donor
rows are valid. The corrected train donor subset contains 3,549 examples / 2,244 genes.

- **B (partner coupling):** L3H1 is the dominant donor-side mediator (+0.114 [0.098,0.130] in the full
  valid subset). In the exact 400-gene closure, L3H1 alone induces +0.0857 [0.0492,0.1280] of behavioral
  B +0.1015; the top 10 induce +0.1007. B is therefore a strong positive-control circuit and is distinct
  from proximal same-type-site perturbation responses.
- **C/D:** their donor head profiles are almost the same (`r=0.804` signed; `r=0.942` magnitude). The
  corrected post-hoc top-three set (L4H12/L3H1/L0H4) has a small exact C>D magnitude excess, but every
  broader set from k=5 onward is inconclusive and the all-head contrast includes zero. This is a discovery
  candidate, not evidence of a competition-specific module; the behavioral specificity gate remains
  failed/instrument-limited.
- **MLPs:** all six MLP writes respond, but no individual/all-MLP C>D magnitude CI excludes zero. Because
  MLP and attention interventions are overlapping cuts through the same sequential computation, their
  effects are not additive.
- **Acceptor targets:** untested at head resolution until the corrected coordinate screen is run. All
  prior acceptor-side head effects, reciprocal nulls, and donor-vs-acceptor comparisons are withdrawn.

The branch is closed without touching chr8/9. The next program step is the wider multi-label atlas in
`RESEARCH_SPINE.md`, not competition confirmation.
