Parameter displacement is not a causal or behavioral result. Smoke runs are instrument checks and may not
promote a claim. GFS4 is about visibility through the derived MLM readout; “fine-tuning erased coupling
from the encoder” is not licensed. GFS8 shows that coupling remains usable by the released classifier;
GFS9 localizes the readable shift descriptively, not causally.
GFS10–GFS18 promote the final-block account to a causal mechanism at bounded resolution; GFS19–GFS20 add
exploratory cross-checkpoint alignment and exact native-unit resolution. GFS21 preserves the failed
shared-index sparse decomposition. GFS22 then closes the prespecified native-unit biological bridge at
its outcome-blind construction gate, before any held-out model result. The strongest
licensed definition is: **the fine-tune reuses inherited period-3 and ≈10.63 positional carriers for
local donor/acceptor evidence while maintaining several distributed, late-integrated sequence signals
carried by multiple attention routes—including cross-endpoint `L5H14`/`L5H8`, donor-selected `L5H12`,
acceptor-selected `L5H11`, and partner-coupling `L5H6`—whose effects are transformed almost entirely
through a mesoscale population of final-MLP units into a compact seed-specific code for splice-classification evidence.** The frozen three-head context cores are
complementary but explain only about one-third of behavior; the surrounding attention computation
remains distributed. Raw low-rank coordinates learned in seed 2020 do not transport at substantial
magnitude across independently fine-tuned seeds, but discovery-only orthogonal alignment transports both
rank-4 causal subspaces and example-conditioned writes on the reused evaluation genes. This does not establish endpoint specialization,
identify a complete or minimal circuit, name a biological grammar in the 32-nt context regions,
reproduce spliceosome biochemistry, make the native units monosemantic, or demonstrate reuse of the base masked-LM circuit.
