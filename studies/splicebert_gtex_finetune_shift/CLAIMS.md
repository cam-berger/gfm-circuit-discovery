Parameter displacement is not a causal or behavioral result. Smoke runs are instrument checks and may not
promote a claim. GFS4 is about visibility through the derived MLM readout; “fine-tuning erased coupling
from the encoder” is not licensed. GFS8 shows that coupling remains usable by the released classifier;
GFS9 localizes the readable shift descriptively, not causally.
GFS10–GFS13 promote the final-block account to a causal mechanism at bounded resolution. The strongest
licensed definition is: **a seed-reproducible, partner-specific, value-leaning final-attention signal
(`L5H6`) is transformed nonlinearly by the final MLP into classifier-aligned donor evidence, while the
signal's source remains distributed rather than localized to the terminal acceptor motif.** This does not
show that `L5H6` alone implements coupling, that the model reproduces spliceosome biochemistry, or that the
fine-tune reuses the base masked-LM circuit.
