Parameter displacement is not a causal or behavioral result. Smoke runs are instrument checks and may not
promote a claim. GFS4 is about visibility through the derived MLM readout; “fine-tuning erased coupling
from the encoder” is not licensed. GFS8 shows that coupling remains usable by the released classifier;
GFS9 localizes the readable shift descriptively, not causally.
GFS10–GFS17 promote the final-block account to a causal mechanism at bounded resolution. The strongest
licensed definition is: **the fine-tune maintains several distributed, late-integrated sequence signals
carried by multiple attention routes—including cross-endpoint `L5H14`/`L5H8`, donor-selected `L5H12`,
acceptor-selected `L5H11`, and partner-coupling `L5H6`—that converge on a shared final-MLP
splice-classification transformation.** This does not establish endpoint specialization, show that these
heads form a complete or minimal circuit, identify a named biological grammar in the 32-nt context
regions, reproduce spliceosome biochemistry, or demonstrate reuse of the base masked-LM circuit.
