# Claims registry — splicebert_partner_selection

One row per empirical claim. **A passing unit test verifies software, never a
scientific claim** — it can advance a claim to `unit-tested`, never to `supported`.
Only a fresh, manifest-stamped artifact advances a claim to `supported`. Downgrades
and retractions are permanent rows; later analyses refine, they do not overwrite.

`pending-fresh-run` · `retired` · `retracted`.

real bases (never `[MASK]`), and every claim carries a matched control.

`exon_vs_intron.json`, `localization.json`, `subspace.json`. **Confirmation on gene-disjoint held-out

## Circuit characterisation (2026-07-12, both-strand data)

All numbers below are **val** (chr11/16/19), on the corrected both-strand dataset (see R7).
Test (chr8/chr9) is opened once, against `PREREGISTRATION_SEALED.md`.

| ID | Claim | Status | Evidence |
|----|-------|--------|----------|
| D12 | **The L2H15 → L3H1 edge is CLOSED.** Nothing of L2H15's contribution reaches the behaviour except through L3H1. | **artifact-supported (val)** | `l2_accounting.json`. Restoring L3H1's head output to its L2H15-intact value, with L2H15 still ablated, rescues **0.959 [0.875, 1.052]** (a CI covering 1.0) of the coupling L2H15 supplies. All 16 layer-3 heads together rescue the same — no other L3 head is involved. The instrument's calibration row (restore the whole layer-2 output) returns **1.000 exactly**, as it must by construction. **Supersedes D11**: the "~60% unaccounted" was an artefact of only ever patching K/V inside a 4-token window at the acceptor. Within that window the rescue is 0.472; L3H1's *whole output* gives 1.05. |
| D13 | **L3H1 WRITES at the 5′ splice site, not at the acceptor it reads.** Its causal contribution is confined to 11 of 1026 tokens. | **artifact-supported (val)** | `position_profile.json` (n=150, six disjoint regions). donor (1 nt) **0.345**; donor flank ±5 (10 nt) **0.495**; acceptor (11 nt) **0.003**; intron body (88 nt) 0.034; upstream exon (319 nt) 0.007; downstream (597 nt) −0.001. All positions: 0.812. The head reads at the acceptor (D7) and writes at the donor neighbourhood — measured, not assumed. This also explains why destroying the donor's *query* collapses donor→partner attention by 95% but costs only 29% of the coupling: it silences one of ~11 redundant query positions reading the same partner. |
| D14 | **The downstream readout is a distributed CHAIN, not a head.** L3H1's write is a signal, not a logit. | **artifact-supported (val)** | `readout_path.json`. Patch L3H1 clean into the corrupt run and clamp every downstream residual write (MLP3, L4-attn, MLP4, L5-attn, MLP5) to its unpatched-corrupt value: the **direct effect is 0.023** of a 0.818 total. No single block mediates (best: MLP3 alone, +0.158). Releasing the stack bottom-up: 0.023 → 0.18 → 0.26 → 0.46 → 0.53 → 0.818. Every link adds; none suffices. There is no compact readout head that we failed to find. |
| D15 | **The completeness test has NO POWER and is reported as inconclusive.** | **artifact-supported (val)** | `completeness_null.json`. Ablating the 47 heads outside C leaves 7.7% of the coupling — but so does ablating 47 random heads (add-one **p = 0.97**); for the 32 layer-4/5 heads, **p = 0.47** (46/100 random sets did as much damage). Any large ablation breaks the LM (health 0.53 → 0.45) and the coupling dies whatever is deleted. At **matched health** the circuit-preserving ablation is *gentler* than random (0.284 vs 0.115). This neither supports nor refutes completeness, and is stated as such rather than read in the flattering direction. **Retracts R12.** |
| D16 | **The read is CONTENT-ADDRESSED and POSITION-INDEPENDENT.** L3H1 locates its partner by the *key vector*, wherever that vector sits — the address is a point in representation space, not a coordinate in the sequence. **Replaces the retracted D9.** | **artifact-supported (val)** | `sealed_val.json::binding`. The head retrieves the partner's content from a position that is not the partner's, purely because the key routed it there. Unlike the swap this retracted, every cell here *can* be zero, and one of them is. |
| D17 | **A decoy AG's content is worth about a third of the read.** The head needs *an* acceptor's content to be findable, and the *partner's specific* content for the rest. | **artifact-supported (val)** | Routing the head to a real decoy AG (partner's **key only** at the decoy) recovers **0.238** [0.187, 0.289] — it attends to the decoy (0.49) and retrieves the decoy's generic "an acceptor is here" value. Against the full in-place read (0.686) that is ~35%. Reported because it was an unexpected non-null: the first draft of the pre-registration assumed this cell would be ~0 and gated on it. ## Retractions (permanent)

| ID | Retracted claim | Date | Reason |
|----|-----------------|------|--------|
| R12 | "Ablating layers 4–5 destroys the coupling (21% retained), so a downstream stage is SPECIFICALLY required." | 2026-07-12 | **Our own claim, made and retracted the same day.** It was collateral damage, not specificity: a size-matched random ablation of 32 heads does as much (add-one p = 0.42; 41/100 random sets at least as damaging), and at matched MLM health the observed set is *gentler* on the coupling than random. The alarm was raised before the null existed. The null is why it did not survive. Superseded by D15 (inconclusive). |
| R11 | Every result produced before 2026-07-12 was implicitly claimed for "internal exons". | 2026-07-12 | **The study was PLUS-STRAND ONLY and never said so.** `coupling.py:326` took `exons[i+1]` as the transcript-order next exon, but `exons` is sorted by *genomic* start — on the minus strand the next exon is `exons[i-1]`. The (correct) minus-strand intron formula applied to the wrong neighbour always yields a negative length, which was nulled, so `next_acceptor_pos` was never assigned and `eligible()` silently dropped **41,153/41,153** minus-strand exons on chr8. Eligible counts roughly doubled on the fix (val 3,118 → 5,930; test 735 → 1,568). **No point estimate was biased** — minus-strand exons couple identically (+1.041 [0.921, 1.168] vs +1.007 [0.899, 1.119]) — and **the circuit is unchanged on the corrected data** (L3H1 #1 at 0.830, L2H15 #2 at 0.515). But every pre-fix number is a plus-strand number, half the sealed split would have been thrown away, and cross-strand generality was asserted rather than tested. |
| R10 | "Held-out coupling is **1.359** [1.266, 1.450]" (D1, as written). | 2026-07-12 | **A conditional mean reported as a behavioural one.** `run_confirm.py:128` keeps only examples with coupling ≥ 0.3 — a filter that is legitimate for a recovery *ratio* (which needs a denominator) and illegitimate for an effect *magnitude*, because it conditions on the outcome. The unconditional effect on the same eligible population is **0.973 [0.919, 1.023]**, median 0.737, with 12.2% of exons at or below zero: a **1.44× inflation with disjoint CIs**. D1's *status* survives (the CI excludes zero by a wide margin); only its magnitude was wrong. Ratios computed on the conditional set (recovery 0.808, the subspace fractions, K×V, the rescues) are **not** affected — that is the use the filter is for — but they must now carry the label "conditional (coupling ≥ 0.3)". |
| R9 | "The matched-random null gives **p = 0.000999** (n=1000)." | 2026-07-12 | **The p-value was a function of the `--n-null` flag, not of the data.** `matched_random_head_sets` drew 1000 single-head sets *with replacement* from a universe of 95. Mean-ablation is deterministic, so those 1000 draws were 95 distinct values duplicated ~10.5×; the add-one p then divided by n+1 = 1001 instead of by the 95 exchangeable units. The reported p is *exactly* 1/1001 and stays 1/(n+1) at n = 60, 10³, 10⁴, 10⁵. The honest exact value is **p = 1/96 = 0.0104** — which is simply the rank-1-of-96 result restated. Still clears α = 0.05; the evidence was **overstated 10.4×**. Fixed: the sampler now de-duplicates, and enumerates outright when the universe is exhaustible. |
| R8 | "MLM health 0.9958 → 0.9953 under ablation, so the head is not simply breaking the model." | 2026-07-12 | **A control that could not fail.** `run_confirm.py:299` took the argmax at 40 random positions that were **never masked** (`task.pair` masks only the donor) and compared it to the base sitting in the input at that position. It scored the model's ability to *copy its own input*. It returns ~0.99 for everything — and **rises to 0.9990 with all 96 attention heads mean-ablated** and the coupling destroyed. It is anti-correlated with model function. It never licensed the claim it was cited for. Re-measured with **masked** probes: baseline **0.545**, C-ablated **0.539** (99% retained), all-heads-ablated 0.295. The conclusion survives; the number and the control do not. |
| R7 | **D9** — "Address and content are SEPARABLE: swapping L3H1's key moves attention to the decoy, swapping its value leaves attention put but destroys the margin, and swapping BOTH leaves attention on the decoy yet fully restores the margin (0% of the effect lost)." | 2026-07-12 | **An algebraic identity, not a measurement.** Attention output is `out_i = Σ_j softmax(q_i·k_j)·v_j`. Transposing the **(key, value) pair** between two positions permutes the summation index `j`: the multiset of (k, v) pairs is unchanged, the softmax denominator is unchanged, and **`out' = out` exactly — for any weights, any data**. So (i) both-swap reproduces the clean logits bit-for-bit (the published `4.655215079` vs `4.655215060` is float noise around an identity), and (ii) key-only and value-only swaps are *provably the same intervention*, which is why their margins agreed to 7 significant figures — a tell that was noticed and rationalised instead of chased. Verified: on a **randomly-initialised** BertForMaskedLM, \|both-swap − baseline\| = **1.2e-06** and the pre-registered gate PASSES. The arm has been deleted and replaced by a **one-way copy** of the partner's key/value into a decoy position, which changes the multiset and can therefore fail. **This was the result the team was told to lead with.** |
| R6 | "L2H15 refines the acceptor's representation — **both** the key that makes it findable **and** the value that makes it readable." | 2026-07-12 | Causally false. The rescue shows **value-only rescue = −0.019** [−0.030, −0.011] (zero) while **key-only = 0.423**, and K+V adds nothing over K alone. The claim was inferred from **cosine similarity**, which ranked the *value* as more perturbed (0.929 vs 0.950) — the exact opposite of the causal result. **Cosine magnitude is not causal relevance.** Superseded by D7. |
| R4 | "L3H1 is **dedicated** to the partner coupling — its measured partner-independent role is ≈ 0." (the second half of D4, as first written) | 2026-07-12 | **Falsified by the same held-out run that confirmed the rest of D4.** The 2×2 factorial on gene-disjoint chromosomes gives a partner-independent local role of **−0.200 [−0.272, −0.128]** — a confidence interval that excludes zero.
