# SpliceBERT positional-proxy modifiability

The study asked whether phase-decoupled continued pretraining weakens SpliceBERT's boundary-register
preference, and whether identical-sequence relocation shows that the learned period-3 positional carrier
causally supports that change. Its frozen protocol was never executed.

It closed at a model-free construction result, PPM1, its only live claim. Under the audited real-module
design, the single-checkpoint `Psi` assay needs 718 blocks, but only 544 remain usable at the required
assignment margin. No cohort was selected and no model was loaded. The controlling artifacts are
`results/splicebert_positional_clock/real_panel_capacity_v1.json` and its independent audit; the
provisional `positional_clock` path is kept because those immutable artifacts record it.

PPM1 is a capacity result, not evidence about SpliceBERT behavior or splicing biology. It does not
resolve the paired `Delta Psi` design, which still needs a separately powered, preregistered study.

Exploratory behavioral and continued-pretraining runs fell outside the protocol, lacked enough
training-seed replication and an independent audit, and backed no claim. They were removed from the
active tree and are recoverable at Git tag `archive/unsupported-exploratory-2026-08-22`.
