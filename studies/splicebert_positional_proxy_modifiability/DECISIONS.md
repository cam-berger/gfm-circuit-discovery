# Positional-proxy modifiability — decisions

## Closure

The study remains closed at PPM1, an independently audited model-free capacity result. The frozen
protocol was never executed, no confirmatory cohort was selected, and no checkpoint was loaded by the
claim-bearing artifacts.

Exploratory behavioral and continued-pretraining work did not satisfy the repository's requirements for
a scientific claim: it ran outside the frozen protocol, used one or two training seeds per arm, and had
no independent audit. Those files were therefore archived and removed from the active tree. The complete
pre-cleanup record is recoverable at Git tag
`archive/unsupported-exploratory-2026-08-22`.

This disposition is not a null result. It leaves the paired `Delta Psi` construction question open and
requires any successor to begin with a new protocol, sufficient training-seed replication, calibration,
and an independent audit before a target result is interpreted.
