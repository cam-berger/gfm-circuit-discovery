# FROZEN R5c preregistration: acceptor-side write and delivery

> **Tier: T2 — provenance.** Supporting record: preregistrations, decision logs, correction ledgers, audits.

> **Status: FROZEN FOR PREFLIGHT ONLY (2026-08-17); TARGET SEALED.** The reviewed protocol,
> normalized instrument, and joint-stack identities are pinned in source. Every output-derived
> predecessor, preflight, target-receipt, and manifest pin remains `None`. Neither scored stage is
> authorized until its exact predecessor packages are independently reviewed and pinned; this
> freeze does not unseal chr8/chr9 or authorize the target.

## 1. Question and claim boundary

R5c asks whether the masked-acceptor behavior established by sealed R1c is
implemented by an exact query-local L3H1 residual write and conditionally
delivered through the prespecified acceptor-side downstream pair.

The two primary questions are:

1. Is the clean-minus-corrupt, post-`W_O` L3H1 contribution at the masked
   acceptor query necessary and sufficient for the paired-donor effect?
2. Conditional on restoring that same write, does L4H11+L4H9 carry the effect
   under both necessity and sufficiency sandwiches, with partner specificity
   and superiority to an intervention-size- and layer-matched random-pair null?

The endpoint is always the hard masked-acceptor-G margin. R5c never constructs
a masked-donor endpoint. The assay supports a causal model-mechanism statement,
not spliceosome chemistry, transcript use, minimality, uniqueness, or a
biological mechanism.

The dense write and primary pair are basis-independent. R5b's low-rank basis is
an optional C2 strengthening only. Its absence or unresolved disposition cannot
invalidate C1 or C4. Singleton heads, the distinct tested set, and the H7 triple
are prespecified diagnostics or later strengthenings and cannot erase an
already supported primary result.

## 2. Temporal design and entry

All R5a, R5b, and R5c methods and model-free registries must be frozen before
R1c target unsealing. The permitted target sequence is:

1. sealed R1c target on chr8/chr9;
2. R5a target on the exact authenticated R1c rows;
3. R5c target on those same rows.

R5c target requires:

- the exact frozen R5c preflight artifact and separate manifest;
- the R1c result, manifest, attempt receipt, completion receipt, predecessor
  preflight artifact, and predecessor preflight manifest authenticated through
  the shared six-file successor verifier;
- an R5a target artifact/manifest with status
  `L3H1_DONOR_LOCAL_READER_SUPPORTED`, the exact capstone bindings, and the
  same checkpoint and R5 stack;
- the terminal pre-unseal R5b disposition artifact/manifest plus its exact
  derivation and validation packages; and
- only when R5b's disposition is `VALIDATED_BASIS`, its authenticated runtime
  NPZ and `gfmcd.r5b.frozen_local_writer_basis/2` contract.

R5b has exactly three accepted terminal dispositions:

- `VALIDATED_BASIS`: execute optional C2 using the authenticated selected basis
  and 32 authenticated random bases;
- `NO_VALIDATED_BASIS_AT_FROZEN_THRESHOLDS`: serialize C2 as not evaluated; or
- `BASIS_INSTRUMENT_UNRESOLVED`: serialize C2 as not evaluated because the R5b
  basis instrument was unresolved.

All three dispositions explicitly leave the dense primary path unaffected.
R3c is not loaded or used as a scientific entry; R5a's authenticated contract
retains only an explicit assertion that R3c was not used as entry evidence.

A healthy negative scientific predecessor is a scientific stop. A malformed
package, failed replay, stack/checkpoint mismatch, hook failure, or health
failure is an instrument failure. The result must keep those dispositions
separate.

## 3. Exact preflight cohort

Preflight is an instrument stage, not a biological result. It uses the exact
authenticated R1c validation-preflight handoff:

- exactly 400 stored rows;
- split `val` and chromosomes chr11, chr16, and chr19;
- exact stored row and gene order;
- no filtering, substitution, or reordering; and
- the handoff's authenticated `P_clean`, `P_under_D`, and `P_under_N` levels.

It also consumes the frozen R5a preflight and the same terminal R5b disposition
that target will consume. It does not require or inspect future R1c target, R5a
target, or R5c target packages. It never opens chr8/chr9 and is never claim
eligible.

Preflight executes the production intervention constructors, basis-conditional
C2 policy, exact family rows, mutation and replay audits, paired hook health,
boundary-binding suite, engineering calibration diagnostics, robust inference
serialization, and exact offline rederivation. Target authenticates and replays
the frozen preflight rather than substituting a rerun.

## 4. Exact target cohort and spend

The target cohort is the exact authenticated 400-row R1c sealed handoff on
chr8/chr9 in stored order. Repeated genes are allowed; at least 30 unique gene
clusters are required. There is no one-row-per-gene requirement.

The outcome-free handoff binds row count, cohort digest, ordered row and gene
digests, sorted unique-gene digest/count, construction digest, source package,
and materialization rules. R5c does not independently select, filter, rank, or
reorder rows.

After target authorization and upstream authentication, R5c writes an
irreversible target-attempt receipt before inspecting the target source rows,
target outcome levels, model/checkpoint path, or output path. The stable spend
key binds stage, checkpoint, exact row cohort, and exact gene cohort. Method or
label changes cannot create another spend.

Only after the receipt may R5c:

1. call the shared authenticated R1c successor common-leg loader;
2. load the chr8/chr9 source examples;
3. materialize all 400 handoff rows; and
4. inspect and stage the checkpoint and output.

Materialization validates all bound coordinates, motifs, calipers,
paired/pseudo constructs, and mutation hygiene and must report that stored order
was preserved with zero filtering/reordering. After immutable result and
manifest publication, R5c writes the completion receipt. If publication
succeeds but completion fails, the biological target remains spent.

## 5. Authenticated R1c common-leg replay

Before any writer or delivery intervention, R5c replays on every same row:

- `P_clean`;
- `P_under_D`, the paired-donor corruption; and
- `P_under_N`, the pseudo-donor corruption.

The target reference arrays come only from
`gfmcd.r5.verified_r1c_target_common_legs/1`, returned by the shared six-file
successor verifier after the R5c receipt. The verified outcome package is never
used for cohort selection or method choice. Preflight uses the outcome-limited
validation handoff instead.

All 400 arrays must reproduce in exact order with absolute and relative
tolerances of `1e-5`. Any mismatch is an instrument failure. No mismatching row
may be dropped, replaced, or reordered.

## 6. Writer variable and optional basis

The primary writer site is
`L3H1.output_projected@acceptor_query`. For each row, R5c isolates the exact
L3H1 additive contribution through the L3 attention output projection:

`context_L3H1(q) @ W_O[:, L3H1_columns].T`.

The shared output-projection bias is excluded. The decision-bearing clean-minus-
corrupt delta is added after `W_O` and before the residual addition, at exactly
one masked-acceptor query token per row. A historical pre-`W_O` head patch is
run only as an equivalence diagnostic; its margin and final residual must agree
with the post-`W_O` intervention within `1e-5`.

Dense sufficiency adds the clean-minus-corrupt contribution to the native
corrupt row. Dense necessity subtracts the same delta from the native clean
row. L0H2 is the fixed direct negative control. L1H12 and L2H15 are not writer
substitutes.

When and only when R5b reports `VALIDATED_BASIS`, R5c broadcasts unchanged to
every row:

- `frozen_basis[k,D]`;
- `random_bases[32,k,D]`;
- `gene_generic_axis[D]`;
- `readout_axis[D]`; and
- scalar `selected_rank`.

The runtime contract binds array dtype/shape/digest, basis and random IDs,
residual-width representation, post-`W_O` writer contract, checkpoint, joint
stack, exact upstream packages, bundle bytes, and target policy. Target
refitting, rank selection, folds, mutation, and redraw are forbidden. If the
basis is not validated, none of these arrays or C2 mutations may be loaded or
executed.

## 7. Delivery sandwiches and controls

Partner and pseudo source arms share bit-identical dense-writer common legs.
For any effect, `specific = partner - pseudo`; target-minus-control superiority
is computed as a direct paired contrast before gene inference.

For each downstream component or ensemble:

- necessity restores the dense writer in the corrupt row and then restores the
  component to its corrupt downstream state; and
- sufficiency transfers the downstream state captured under dense-writer
  restoration into the native corrupt row.

The primary delivery ensemble is L4H11+L4H9. For both necessity and
sufficiency it must be materially positive on partner and specificity,
equivalent on pseudo, and superior on direct partner and direct specificity
gaps to the mean of eight fixed all-L4 random pairs. This C4 matched-pair mean
is the only random-family veto in R5c.

Structured route controls remain decision-bearing:

- C3 compares each of L4H11, L4H9, and L4H7 to each of L4H0, L4H2, and L5H11;
- the C4 distinct-tested-set strengthening requires all three fixed route pairs
  to be equivalent and the primary pair to exceed each on partner and
  specificity; and
- C5 compares L4H11+L4H9+L4H7 to the fixed route triple containing the
  prespecified route controls.

C3 singleton results are diagnostics. C5's matched random-triple mean is stored
as descriptive geometry only and cannot veto its structured H7 strengthening.
C2 target random-gap geometry is descriptive; the R5b disposition owns the
frozen basis/random-null validation and C2 cannot veto C1 or C4.

## 8. Closed families

The exact decision matrices are:

| Family | Rows | Role |
|---|---:|---|
| C1 | 9 | dense necessity/sufficiency across partner, pseudo, specificity; L0H2 direct control |
| C2 | 10 | optional selected-basis necessity/sufficiency plus selected-minus-mean-random geometry |
| C3 | 72 | singleton sandwiches, structured route controls, and direct partner/specific gaps |
| C4 | 40 | primary/route pair sandwiches, structured gaps, and the matched random-pair mean gap |
| C5 | 20 | H7/route triple sandwiches, structured gaps, and descriptive random-triple mean gaps |

C2 is absent from execution and serialized with its exact R5b-derived skip
disposition unless R5b validated the basis. The other four matrices remain
complete and valid.

One-, two-, and three-head controls are never pooled. The deterministic random
registries exclude registered tested heads and preserve intervention size and
layer composition. C3's three alternative singleton paths receive Holm
correction together. Other paths are singleton or nested fixed-sequence tests.
Eight L4+L5 random pairs and eight L4+L4+L5 random triples remain authenticated
descriptive/health primitives only; they enter no decision matrix or veto.

## 9. Decision statistics

The scientific decision authority is the conjunction of the unweighted gene
mean and prespecified 10%-trimmed gene mean under studentized gene-cluster
bootstrap inference.

- Rows are aggregated within gene before inference.
- Genes receive equal weight, so repeated rows do not inflate the independent
  unit count.
- All decision rows in a family share one whole-gene resample matrix.
- Production uses `B=10,000` bootstrap draws and seed `0`.
- The bootstrap is explicitly asymptotic, never described as exact.
- The 4,000-draw boundary sign-flip calculation is serialized as a diagnostic
  with `decision_authority=false`.

The common SESOI is `0.05` hard-margin units. Material-positive and direct
target-minus-control requirements need strict lower bounds above `+0.05`.
Equivalence requires both TOST edges for `[-0.05,+0.05]`. Every constituent of
a path must pass its raw and trimmed boundary and the path's adjusted p-value
must be strictly below `0.05`. Touching a boundary fails.

Records also include median, fixed quantiles, boundary-exceedance prevalence,
and a direction-aware top-1% deletion estimate. Those diagnose tails but never
replace the raw-and-trimmed conjunction.

## 10. Capability and health

Before scientific classification, R5c must establish:

- one masked-acceptor-G readout and no masked-donor endpoint;
- full-logit and hard-margin replay for every captured condition;
- dense final-residual replay;
- L3H1 and all registered-head query self-patch no-ops;
- exact query-only, head-exact/post-`W_O` mutations with no off-mask changes;
- bit-identical writer common legs across every delivery arm;
- exact ensemble membership and patch size;
- a coherent R5b disposition: 33 authenticated projectors and energy-matched,
  never-redrawn random arms if C2 executes, or zero C2 arrays/mutations if it
  does not;
- passing boundary-binding and observed-centered robust sensitivity suites;
- complete paired hook-health noninferiority; and
- no candidate outcomes used for capability, selection, or method choice.

Engineering calibration is recorded and replayed as a non-authorizing
diagnostic. It cannot open or close target.

Hook health uses up to the first 16 stored rows and two deterministic masked
non-site nucleotide probes per row. Every registered necessity/sufficiency arm
is constructed exactly as in production and compared with its own same-state
baseline: dense C1 necessity against clean and sufficiency against corrupt;
every delivery singleton, pair, triple, matched-random pair, and matched-random
triple necessity against the post-`W_O` writer-restored state and sufficiency
against corrupt; and, only for `VALIDATED_BASIS`, selected plus all 32
energy-matched random C2 projector necessities against clean and sufficiencies
against corrupt. A nonvalidated R5b disposition executes and registers no C2
health arm. The serialized arm registry, constructor labels, baseline states,
and both source-leg record sets must have exact coverage. The one-sided
paired-harm upper confidence bound must be strictly below `0.10` for every arm.
There is no inclusive boundary and no absolute-accuracy floor. Failure is an
instrument failure, never a biological null.

## 11. Ordered interpretation

The fixed sequence is:

1. authenticated R1c paired-donor behavior;
2. authenticated R5a donor-local reader;
3. R5c dense local write;
4. R5c primary pair delivery;
5. optional low-rank strengthening or its exact R5b-derived not-evaluated
   disposition;
6. optional distinct-tested-set strengthening; and
7. optional H7 triple strengthening.

A healthy negative reached gate is a scientific stop; an unhealthy gate is an
instrument stop. Later gates are marked not tested according to the stop type.
The two exact R5b-derived C2 not-evaluated dispositions are not reached gates:
C2 is recorded as not evaluated with `tested_here=false`, while the already-run
distinct-tested-set and H7 gates retain their own results and ordered
interpretation. A healthy negative C2 after `VALIDATED_BASIS` remains a
scientific stop. The primary claim requires only supported C1 and C4, including
C4's matched random-pair veto. No C2, C3, or C5 result can weaken that supported
claim.

## 12. Runtime, replay, and immutable provenance

Scored execution is MPS-only. `device` must resolve to `mps`, and
`PYTORCH_ENABLE_MPS_FALLBACK` must be absent; even a value of `0` is forbidden.
A CPU or fallback run is a different, unauthorized instrument.

Authorization occurs before resolving any user-supplied scored-stage path. Pins
are literal non-wildcard SHA-256 values. R5c's R1c pins must exactly equal the
canonical shared R1c pins. Upstream artifacts and manifests must be clean,
independently bound, and byte-exact.

The result stores primitive partner/pseudo legs, raw family matrices,
gene-sufficient statistics, robust inference and diagnostic hashes, mutation
audits, condition captures, R1c common-leg replay, R5b disposition, upstream
chain, stack, checkpoint, cohort/materialization identity, runtime identity,
exact command/parameters/seeds, target receipt, and immutable publication
digests. In-memory target replay rederives capability, matrices, raw and
trimmed inference, path classifications, structured-control activity, verdict,
top-level status, ordered gate dispositions, and claim eligibility. Claim
eligibility is membership in the closed set of supported R5c dispositions, not
a string-prefix test.

The public model/genome-free target-package verifier opens exactly the result,
manifest, target-attempt receipt, and completion receipt. It authenticates every
file identity and crosslink, requires the manifest to bind the exact result and
attempt, replays the receipt biological target and preflight/upstream context,
requires the completion to bind the exact result/manifest/attempt, reruns the
full in-memory scientific replay, and requires the completion's official
disposition to equal the rederived verdict. Any missing, stale, repackaged, or
inconsistent member fails closed and licenses no scientific claim. Inputs are
snapshotted before execution and verified afterward; result and manifest are
created without overwrite, and completion is written only after publication.
