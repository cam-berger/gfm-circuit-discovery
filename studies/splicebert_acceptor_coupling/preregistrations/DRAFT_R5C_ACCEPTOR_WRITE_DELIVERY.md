clean `L3H1` to the paired-donor-corrupt run write `U_A`, and do fixed late-head candidates carry that

## 4. Primitive states and the direct L3H1 leg

For each partner-source row:

- `m-` is the native margin under paired-donor corruption;
- `c+` is the **same corrupt run** with the clean same-example `L3H1` masked-acceptor head output
  restored; and
- `m(c+)` is its native acceptor margin.

Thus direct L3H1 rescue is `R_L3 = m(c+) - m-`. `c+` is not the native clean run, a final-residual
plant, a cross-example transplant, or a re-executed clean sequence. Repeat the identical construction
for the matched pseudo-donor source. Apply `partner_pseudo_specific` to obtain partner, pseudo, and
specific direct-rescue endpoints.

The size-matched direct-head control replaces `L0H2` at the same acceptor-query output using its own
clean/corrupt same-example captures. It is a failable model-path control; an architecture-forced
upstream invariance is not used as evidence.

## 5. Does L3H1 write `U_A`?

Let `h-` be the final masked-acceptor residual under the native corrupt run and `h^{L3+}` the final
residual produced by `c+`. Let `P_U` be the selected-rank, cross-fitted R5b projector for that row.
Use `l3_to_u_mediation_estimands` on four margins:

1. `m- = m(h-)`;
2. `m(h^{L3+}) = m(c+)`;
3. `m(P_U[h^{L3+}] into h-)`, implemented as
   `m(h- + P_U(h^{L3+}-h-))`; and
4. `m(replace U_A in h^{L3+} with h-)`, implemented as
   `m(h^{L3+} - P_U(h^{L3+}-h-))`.

The frozen estimands are

`S_U = m(P_U[h^{L3+}] into h-) - m-`,

`N_U = m(h^{L3+}) - m(replace its U_A component with h-)`,

with `R_L3` reported separately. Repeat for the pseudo source and compute partner-minus-pseudo
specificity for `S_U` and `N_U`. Necessity and sufficiency are not averaged or divided by `R_L3` for
inference.

Thirty-two deterministic random projectors match the selected rank and, per row, the energy of
`P_U(h^{L3+}-h-)`. They receive the same two interchange directions. No random vector is redrawn,
zero-filled, or rescaled globally.

## 6. Conditional downstream delivery sandwich

For downstream head or head set `d`, capture:

- `d-`: its acceptor-query output under the native corrupt run; and
- `d^{c+}`: its acceptor-query output under `c+`.

Use `delivery_sandwich_estimands` with the exact common legs:

`N_d = m(c+) - m(c+ with d <- d-)`,

`S_d = m(m- run with d <- d^{c+}) - m-`.

The necessity leg therefore asks whether `d` is needed **inside the L3H1-rescued computation**. The
sufficiency leg asks whether the state `d` develops under `c+` can carry support into the native
corrupt baseline. Generic clean ablation, clean-versus-corrupt head effects, and direct head
restoration outside these common legs are different estimands and cannot enter this decision.

Every candidate and donor-route comparator uses byte-identical `m-` and `m(c+)` arrays. The helper
rejects a candidate-versus-route gap if either common leg differs. For each component, repeat partner
and pseudo source legs and derive:

- `N_partner`, `S_partner`;
- `N_pseudo`, `S_pseudo`; and
- `N_specific = N_partner-N_pseudo`, `S_specific = S_partner-S_pseudo`.

For all 3 x 3 candidate/donor-route pairs, compute on the identical **partner-source** legs:

`G_N(c,r)=N_c-N_r` and `G_S(c,r)=S_c-S_r`.

These are raw row-level partner gaps. The pseudo and partner-minus-pseudo specific endpoints remain
separate corrected rows for each component; there is no second caller-supplied gap endpoint. Using
the partner source here preserves the literal common-leg check implemented by
`delivery_candidate_route_gaps`. A ratio to total L3 rescue is descriptive only.

Every stored L3-to-`U_A` and downstream-sandwich record contains primitive margin cells and derived
estimands. Before any family matrix is constructed, the production runner projects away only the
separately validated audit metadata and invokes the exact core replayers
`replay_l3_to_u_mediation_record` and `replay_delivery_sandwich_record`. They reconstruct every
estimand solely from primitive cells and require bit-identical float64 digests. The L0H2 direct
control is replayed analogously from its native-corrupt and L0H2-restored cells. A stored `N_d`,
`S_d`, `N_U`, `S_U`, direct rescue, specificity row, or gap cannot enter inference if it disagrees
with the primitive cells. Candidate-control gaps consume only canonical replayed sandwiches.

## 9. Frozen decisions

### 9.1 L3H1 writes the acceptor-side state

`L3H1_WRITES_U_A_SUPPORTED` requires:

1. direct L3H1 partner rescue and specificity material positive, pseudo equivalent, and L0H2 control
   equivalent;
2. `S_U` and `N_U` partner and specificity material positive, pseudo equivalent; and
3. every target-minus-random `S_U/N_U` contrast has a simultaneous lower bound above zero.

Sufficiency-only or necessity-only results are named as such and do not license “writes.”
A resolved active pseudo or `L0H2` arm is `NONSELECTIVE_OR_CONTROL_ACTIVE`; failure to beat healthy
rank/energy-matched projectors is `WRITE_NOT_DISTINGUISHED_FROM_MATCHED_NULLS`. These are scientific
rejections. Boundary-touching write constituents are `SCIENTIFICALLY_UNRESOLVED`; only failed
plumbing, health, invariant, hash, or calibration-capability gates are `INSTRUMENT_UNRESOLVED`.

### 9.2 Delivery distinct within the tested downstream-head set

`TESTED_HEAD_SET_DISTINCT_DELIVERY_SUPPORTED` requires all of:

1. primary `L4H11+L4H9` ensemble `N_partner`, `S_partner`, `N_specific`, and `S_specific` material
   positive; both pseudo endpoints equivalent;
2. every one of the three donor-route pairs is equivalent on all six raw endpoints: partner, pseudo,
   and specific, separately for `N` and `S`;
3. the primary-pair `G_N` and `G_S` are material positive against each of the three donor-route pairs
   and every frozen random pair;
4. the secondary acceptor triple has material-positive partner and specific `N/S` and equivalent
   pseudo `N/S`; the donor-route triple is equivalent on partner, pseudo, and specific `N/S`; and the
   acceptor-triple `G_N/G_S` are material positive against the donor triple and every random triple;
5. at least one of `L4H11`, `L4H9`, or `L4H7` has material-positive partner and specific `N/S` and
   equivalent pseudo `N/S`; and
6. each of `L4H0`, `L4H2`, and `L5H11` is equivalent on all six singleton endpoints--partner,
   pseudo, and specific for both `N` and `S`--with the surviving candidate's `G_N/G_S` material
   positive against all three.

Thus every donor-route singleton, pair, and triple must independently establish strict equivalence
for partner, pseudo, and specific on both sandwich directions. Partner/pseudo equivalence alone is
insufficient.

If both candidate and donor-route ensembles mediate, the result is `MIXED_OR_SHARED_DELIVERY`, not
distinct. A resolved active pseudo arm is `NONSELECTIVE_DELIVERY`; failure to exceed healthy frozen
random ensembles is `NOT_DISTINGUISHED_FROM_MATCHED_NULLS`. These are valid scientific rejections.
If candidate necessity passes but sufficiency fails, or vice versa, report the one-sided result
without “delivery route.” Boundary-touching scientific intervals are `SCIENTIFICALLY_UNRESOLVED`;
failed hook isolation, health, hashes, or classifier capability are `INSTRUMENT_UNRESOLVED`.
