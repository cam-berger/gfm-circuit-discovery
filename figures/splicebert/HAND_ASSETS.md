## Do not cite the binding artwork

The donor–acceptor binding illustration asserts a content-addressed, position-independent read. That interpretation is retracted as R15: moving a key/value pair that already contains absolute position information makes the apparent relocation result architecturally forced.

Use [`../../studies/splicebert_partner_selection/FINDINGS.md`](../../studies/splicebert_partner_selection/FINDINGS.md) for the current mechanism. It supports a conjunctive acceptor read and a period-3 positional contribution, but no pointer or content-addressed lookup claim.
