The complete layer-5 context-head census is:

- `native_context_head_census_confirmation_v1.json`: fresh, gene-disjoint `n=200` confirmation across

These artifacts control GFS17. They establish shared context carriers `L5H14`/`L5H8`, a donor-selected
`L5H12` assignment, and an acceptor-selected `L5H11` assignment under bidirectional one-head
interventions. Their positive descriptive opposite-endpoint measurements do not license specialization.

The joint/path/low-rank extension is:

- `native_context_lowrank_discovery_v1.json`: fresh seed-2020 gene means and frozen endpoint SVD bases,
  task directions, ranks, and random-subspace tags;
- `native_context_joint_lowrank_confirmation_v1.json`: untouched `n=200`, five-seed joint-head,
  head→MLP path, and low-rank confirmation.

These artifacts control GFS18. The frozen triples confirm above three matched-size control sets and carry
`28.6–34.4%` of behavior, so they are a structured but incomplete causal core. Their joint effects are
mildly super-additive rather than redundant. Path patching places `98.0–100.0%` of the triple effect
through the induced final-MLP write. The seed-2020 task direction does not confirm across all seeds, and
no tested rank through 64 reaches 50% of the full-MLP effect; raw low-rank coordinates are therefore
bounded as seed-specific rather than licensed as a universal classifier direction.

The exploratory coordinate-alignment extension is:

- `native_context_per_seed_alignment_discovery_v1.json` plus `.npz`: five seed-specific bases and

- `native_context_per_seed_alignment_evaluation_v1.json` plus `.npz`: raw/aligned basis and

These artifacts control GFS19. Aligned rank-4 bases and example-conditioned writes recover nearly the
complete MLP effect; raw coordinates do not. The reused GFS18 partitions keep this exploratory.

The exact native-unit extension is:

- `native_context_final_mlp_unit_discovery_v1.json`: every post-GELU unit tested individually; nested
  context/head-induced groups and output-norm controls frozen per seed and endpoint;
- `native_context_final_mlp_unit_evaluation_v1.json` plus `.npz`: completeness curves, exact
  head→unit paths, and descriptive singleton ownership.

These artifacts control GFS20. Both context effects first reach 80% with 256 of 2,048 native units.
The acceptor triple's induced path reaches 80% with 64 units, the donor triple with 256, and the
head-responsive top-64 groups overlap whole-context top-64 groups by 84–86%. This is a mesoscale
overcomplete implementation of the compact output code, not evidence that any unit is a monosemantic
biological feature.

The biological-feature sparse-instrument arm is:

- `sparse_transcoder_sequence_package_v1.json`: strict label-free sequence states with source hashes;
- `sparse_transcoder_activation_capture_v1.json` plus `.npz`: exact base and five-seed final-MLP
  input/write views;
- `sparse_transcoder_training_attempt1_failure.json`: preserved inactive-threshold diagnostic failure;
- `sparse_transcoder_training_v1.json` plus `.pt`: the complete frozen grid and unresolved verdict.

These artifacts control GFS21. The planted sparse detector/writer control passes, but no joint setting
passes the all-view write-delta reconstruction and initialization-stability gates. Every joint fit
leaves all fine-tuned shared and tuned-only blocks inactive. The behavioral preflight was not opened,
so the artifact licenses no sparse-feature identity, causal patch, or steering claim. This bounds the
specified shared-index sparse parameterization; it does not negate the native-unit mechanism or show
that fine-tuning learned no biological feature.

The exact-dose biological confirmation construction is:

- `native_biological_features_confirmation_construction_v1.json`: 6,400 outcome-blind low/high
  candidate/control sequence states on 200 frozen held-out genes, with an adjacent manifest.

This artifact controls GFS22 and has status `STOPPED_MINIMUM_COHORT`. Complete candidate-plus-three-
control cohorts contain only 23 donor/DAZAP1, 33 donor/SFPQ, 22 acceptor/UNK, and 21 acceptor/DAZAP1
genes after exact low/high edit-dose and nuisance matching, below the frozen 60-example/45-gene gate.
No checkpoint was loaded and no confirmation output was read. The downstream native-unit, steering,
external-candidate, and reporter-outcome stages therefore remain closed.
