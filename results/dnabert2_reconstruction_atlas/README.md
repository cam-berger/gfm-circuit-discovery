# DNABERT-2 Reconstruction Atlas results

> **Tier: T1 — canonical.**

The bounded GENCODE-v1, multisource-v4, and context-margin-v1 results and their preflights are
indexed in `studies/dnabert2_reconstruction_atlas/RESULTS.md`. Context-margin v1 confirms
target-preserving proximal-context effects for nine classes under a strict DNABERT-2
geometry-qualified alternative margin. The promoter-classifier sequence now also includes a
replicated +11 to +21 nominal behavioral source region, while regional late-pair V localization
and independent promoter-feature qualification remain unsupported. A post-hoc performance
follow-up shows a repeated AUROC loss and 7/231 real-promoter label flips, but no net balanced
accuracy loss because pseudo-TSS corrections offset target errors. These are model-behavior
results, not cellular function or original-GUE performance. Bulk collections, variants, per-site
scores, candidate pools, and hook activations are reproducible runtime data and remain ignored.
